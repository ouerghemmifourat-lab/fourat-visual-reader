A flowchart with six steps and one decision, one entry point, one exit, from order received to delivered. It takes an order through a stock check to shipping.

Step one, order received. Step two, check stock. Decision: in stock? If yes, step three, pack. If no, step four, back-order, then back to step two.

After packing, step five, ship. Step six, delivered.

Steps two and four form a loop until stock is available.
