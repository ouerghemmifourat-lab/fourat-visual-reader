A flowchart with six steps and one decision, two entry points, one exit, from web order and phone order to done. It validates an order, checks payment, and fulfils it.

Step one, web order. Step two, phone order. Both join step three, validate order. Decision: payment OK? If yes, step four, fulfil, then step six, done. If no, step five, contact customer, then back to step three.

Steps three and five form a loop until payment is accepted.
